Schedule, Prep, Capture

One companion for the whole journey, not two separate tools.

1
Schedule
Where should I go?

It understands your existing schedule, your interests, the events around you, and, if you allow it, where you are. Then it helps you decide which events and meetings actually fit.

2
Prep
Who am I meeting, and what should we talk about?

Before an event or a coffee chat, it prepares the relevant people, your common ground, recent context, and suggested questions. You walk in already warm.

3
Capture
Who did I meet, and what did we say?

Afterward, it automatically remembers the relationships you build, so the next conversation with the same person picks up where the last one ended.
